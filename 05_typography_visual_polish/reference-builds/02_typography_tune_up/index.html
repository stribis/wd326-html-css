Independent electronic artist

Signals After Midnight

Nova Vale turns late trains, overheard conversations, and field recordings into compact electronic songs.

Artist profile

Finding rhythm in ordinary places

The project began with recordings made on platforms, inside station halls, and along quiet streets after the final tram. Those sounds are not decoration: they establish pace, texture, and a sense of place before the first synthesizer arrives.

The visual identity follows the same approach. Repeated type sizes, deliberate spacing, and a limited measure give each story a recognizable rhythm without making every paragraph look identical.

Reading should feel effortless

Typography creates hierarchy, but it also affects whether a reader can comfortably stay with an article. Line length, line height, contrast, and spacing matter as much as choosing an interesting typeface.
